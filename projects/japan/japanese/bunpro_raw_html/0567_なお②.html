About なお②

When used at the beginning of a sentence, 尚(なお) 'furthermore' takes on the role of a conjunction, and will often carry the meaning of 'in addition', 'furthermore', 'even more', or 'moreover'. This use is regularly seen after the previous sentence or clause has expressed some base fact or situation that the second sentence will then add to. Especially when the information expressed in the (B) part of the sentence is surprising or shocking in some way.

This structure will almost always appear without the kanji being used.

As 尚(なお) is a formal grammar structure, it will most frequently appear in letters, posters, commercials and other similar material.

Fun-fact

In all of its uses, なお simply indicates that taking the previous information of (A) into account, although (B) may not be expected, it is in fact true. This can be used either positively or negatively, and just highlights (B) as exceeding expectations.

Examples

--:--

    明日(あした)は臨時(りんじ)休業(きゅうぎょう)です。尚(なお)、定休(ていきゅう)日(び)は水曜日(すいようび)です。

    Tomorrow is a temporary holiday. In addition, Wednesday is our set store holiday.

    作品(さくひん)の提出(ていしゅつ)は今月(こんげつ)いっぱいまで。尚(なお)、テーマは各自(かくじ)で考(かんが)えること。

    The window for turning in your work is through the end of the month. In addition, you should think about your theme individually.

    只今(ただいま)パンを焼(や)いております。尚(なお)、出来上(できあ)がりは１１(じゅういち)時(じ)を予定(よてい)しております。

    Bread is currently being baked. In addition, it is scheduled to be ready at 11.

    説明(せつめい)は以上(いじょう)です。尚(なお)、手続(てつづ)きは明後日(みょうごにち)までにお願(ねが)いします。

    This concludes the explanation. In addition, please do the formalities by the day after tomorrow.

    では、これで試験(しけん)を終(お)わります。尚(なお)、点数(てんすう)は追(お)って発表(はっぴょう)いたします。

    Well, this concludes the test. In addition, I will announce the score afterwards.
