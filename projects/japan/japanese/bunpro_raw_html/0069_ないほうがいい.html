About ないほうがいい

Like たほうがいい, ないほうがいい is used in Japanese for giving advice. However, this expression suggests that it is better 'not' to do something.

This phrase is constructed by using the negative form of a verb grouped with 方(ほう), the particle が, and the い-Adjective, いい.

As 方(ほう) can be translated closely to 'way', or 'direction', this expression is suggesting that one specific choice is simply the better 'way'.

This expression is quite direct, and can imply that there may be some sort of negative consequence if the (A) verb is done.

Topics

That Way Is Good

N5 Topic

In this Topic, we're going to learn how to give advice about what is better to do (or not) using the phrase ほうがいい. This phrase can be used in combination with verbs in a few different forms, with different forms implying different things.

Examples

--:--

    その肉(にく)を食(た)べないほうがいい。

    It'd be better not to eat that meat.

    タバコを吸(す)わないほうがいい。

    It would be better not to smoke cigarettes.

    汚(よご)れないほうがいい。

    It would be better not to get dirty.

    一人(ひとり)で行(い)かないほうがいい。

    It would be better not to go alone.

    毎日(まいにち)肉(にく)を食(た)べないほうがいい。

    It would be better not to eat meat every day.
