About っぱなし

っぱなし is a suffix that originally comes from the う - Verb 放(はな)す 'to let loose'. When attached to the conjunctive form of verbs, it is used to indicate that something has been 'left' in a certain state. っぱなし is almost exclusively used negatively.

Fun Fact

Although 放(はな)す means 'to let loose', っぱなし conveys a feeling that is closer to 'being unchecked'. This means っぱなし can be used to convey that 'the act of (A)' is continuing without anyone there to stop it, or that the 'state of (A)' is continuing without anyone there to stop it. Which meaning is conveyed will depend mostly on the (A) verb.

Examples

--:--

    誰(だれ)かがそのドアを開(あ)けっ放(ぱな)しにした。

    Someone left the door open.

    ライトをつけっ放(ぱな)しにしないで。

    Don't leave the light on.

    電話(でんわ)が鳴(な)りっ放(ぱな)しだけど…大丈夫(だいじょうぶ)？

    Your phone keeps ringing... Is that not a problem?

    いつも開(あ)けっ放(ぱな)しで、ちゃんと閉(し)めてよね。

    You always leave it open, make sure you close it ok?

    水(みず)を出(だ)しっ放(ぱな)しにするのはやめてください。

    Please stop leaving the water on.
