Structure

Number + Counter + 強(きょう)

Details

    About 強

    強(きょう) is a suffix which is used to say that something is a little more than a certain figure. When used in this way, it will be directly attached to a counter in the pattern number + counter + 強(きょう).

    As 強(きょう) literally means 'strong', it implies the number is 'strong' enough to surpass the count. It will usually imply a very small margin.

    Caution

    A minority of speakers, especially younger speakers, will interpret 弱(じゃく) as meaning a time a little over that number, with 強(きょう) being interpreted as a time that is quite a lot over that number. Despite this, most speakers would still interpret 強(きょう) as meaning 'a little over'.

    This is because they are interpreting 弱(じゃく) as the time 'plus a little more', and 強(きょう) as the number 'plus a lot more'.

    This interpretation is still widely considered wrong, so in formal or test settings (such as the JLPT) 強(きょう) will not be used in this way.

    Examples

    --:--

      マネージャー：「この会場(かいじょう)のキャパは何人(なんにん)だ？」チーフ：「大体(だいたい)３００人(さんびゃくにん)強(きょう)が入(はい)ります！」

      Manager: 'What is the capacity of this venue?' Chief: 'It fits roughly just more than 300 people!'

        この荷物(にもつ)は１０(じゅっ)キロ強(きょう)ある。

        This luggage weighs just more than 10 kilograms.

          二日酔(ふつかよ)いのせいで、１２時間(じゅうにじかん)強(きょう)も寝(ね)続(つづ)けていた。

          Because of a hangover, I kept sleeping for a little more than 12 hours.

            隣町(となりまち)の人口(じんこう)は１，０００(いっせん)人(にん)強(きょう)しかいない。

            The population of the neighboring town is only just more than 1,000 people.

              相手(あいて)チームのエースは身長(しんちょう)２３０(にひゃくさんじゅっ)センチ強(きょう)もあるそうだ。

              I heard the opposing team's ace is just a little more than 230 centimeters tall.
