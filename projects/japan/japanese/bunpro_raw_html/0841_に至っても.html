About に至っても

In contrast to に至(いた)っては, which presents the most extreme examples for something from within a specific category. に至(いた)っても will present the same extreme example from which some result will not occur. This is due to the adverbial particle も 'even' replacing は. に至(いた)っても may be translated as 'even if it came to (A), (B)', 'even when (A), (B)'. (B) will often be negative, thus highlighting that even the extreme (A) couldn't affect (B).

This is a combination of the case-marking particle に, the て-form of the る-Verb 至(いた)る 'to climax', or 'to culminate in', and the adverbial particle も.

に至(いた)っても will appear following nouns, or verbs in their dictionary form.

に至(いた)っても will often be used when the speaker laments or regrets that despite reaching the point of (A), (B) is something that will still not, or did not change. However, it may occasionally be used positively, especially when the speaker is expressing their determination.

Examples

--:--

    彼(かれ)は病院(びょういん)に来(く)るに至(いた)っても、まだ治療(ちりょう)をすることを拒否(きょひ)していた。

    He was still refusing medical treatment, even when it came to the point of him coming to the hospital.

    就業時間(しゅうぎょうじかん)を過(す)ぎるに至(いた)っても、この会議(かいぎ)は終(お)わりそうにありません。

    Even now when work hours will pass, this meeting is showing no signs of ending.

    結婚(けっこん)するに至(いた)っても、完全(かんぜん)にお互(たが)いを理解(りかい)し合(あ)えないカップルが多(おお)い。

    Many couples can't understand each other completely even when they are about to get married.

    小説(しょうせつ)：「だが、事(こと)ここに至(いた)っても、まだ謝(あやま)ることを拒否(きょひ)するほど頑固(がんこ)である」。

    Novel: 'However, even when things have come to this, he is still obstinate to the point that he refuses to apologize.'

    記事(きじ)：「首相(しゅしょう)は政府(せいふ)の腐敗(ふはい)を暴露(ばくろ)されるに至(いた)っても、まだ詳細(しょうさい)情報(じょうほう)を隠(かく)し通(とお)そうとしていた。」

    Article: 'Even when the corruption of the government was exposed, the prime minister still tried to hide the details.'
