# Compound Particles in Japanese — 複合助詞 | Japanese Grammar
## In Brief
Japanese compound particles are fixed combinations such asにおいて,に対して,について,によって,にとって, andをもとに. They connect a noun to the rest of a sentence and make relationships such as “in a setting,” “toward a target,” “about a topic,” “by a means,” “from someone’s viewpoint,” or “based on a source” explicit. They are especially common in news, academic prose, business communication, and formal presentations.
## Overview
A particle is a short grammatical marker placed after a word or phrase. Acompound particle(a multi-part expression functioning much like one particle) is longer than a basic particle such as は, が, を, or に. Many compound particles developed from an ordinary particle plus a verb or noun, but frequent combinations now behave as recognizable units: 会議において“at the conference,” 調査について“about the survey,” and 資料をもとに“based on the materials.”
